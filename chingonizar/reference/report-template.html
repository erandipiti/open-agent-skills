What is costing you today

Five things that cost you customers.

  • The contact email sends messages to the template address

    The Contact page shows your real address, but tapping it opens an email addressed to mailto:info@mysite.example, the builder's sample address. Anyone writing from there never reaches you.

    Fix this first: it is the only visible way to contact you on a phone.

  • There is no quick way to ask for a quote

    The site has 0 WhatsApp links and 0 phone links. The home page has 16 words and no button to reach you. The menu is four icons with no text.

    Your social profile says "Info by DM". Someone arriving from a phone does not know where to go next.

  • Template pages are published and search engines can see them

    There are 11 sample products ("Sample product", with a buy button) and pages with filler text such as This is a Paragraph.

    Someone who lands on one of these pages from a search thinks the site is abandoned.

  • The shop page contradicts itself

    One page offers Rent for 70 and another says We have no products to show right now.

    It depends on whether the shop is still running. It is listed under open points.

  • The site fell behind your social profile

    The footer says © 2023 by Example Co. and the newest dated project is from 2020. On Instagram we saw 12 posts observed between 2026-01-02 and 2026-01-10.

    What you offer today does not appear on the site.

Before we meet

Open points.

There is no right answer. These are decisions only you can make, and they change what we recommend. Think them over; we will build the final proposal from them.

How do you want people to reach you?

Today the site offers one email that does not work, and your profile says "Info by DM". A new customer needs one clear path, and someone answering there.

  • Who answers messages and how fast can they do it?
  • Do you prefer WhatsApp, a form that goes to your email, or direct messages?

Is the shop still running?

Some pages offer products and others say there is nothing to show.

  • If it runs: what is sold, at what price, and how often?
  • If not, we remove it and the site gets cleaner.

What do you really want to sell?

Search results read a short phrase taken from the search listing and your profile describes something different. The Services page needs a closed list.

  • Which services bring you the most and which do you enjoy most?
  • Which clients or collaborations can we name with their permission?

What we reviewed

Scope and pending items.

  • Of the addresses in the site map, 15 of 28 are template or draft pages. We checked every internal link: 0 broken.

  • On a phone, the home page takes 4.9 s to show the main content; on desktop, 1.2 s. This is a lab measurement: no real-visitor data is published for a site this size.

  • We did not submit the contact form. It needs to be tested together to know whether messages arrive.

  • We did not find a map listing for the business. If one exists, send us the link.

  • The domain has no email security records (No SPF, No DMARC). It does not matter today if you use a webmail address; it will if you ever use an address on your own domain.
